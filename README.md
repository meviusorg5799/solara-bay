Solara Bay — One Last Getaway: a 2D top-down open-world crime game. Retired getaway driver Mara "Rook" Voss is pulled back for one last job across a sun-soaked coastal city — steal cars, outrun a 5-star police response, and run five story missions from the first stolen wheels to a dawn escape at the airfield.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network, no assets to download — everything is drawn procedurally and every sound is synthesized with WebAudio.

## How to play

- **WASD / arrows** — walk or drive · **E** — steal a parked car / get out · **SPACE** — shoot (pistol on foot, drive-by from the car) · **SHIFT** — sprint
- **P / ESC** — pause · **M** — mute · **T** — pick up a taxi fare · **R** — repair at the safehouse ($50)
- **Wanted 0–5 stars**: patrol cars chase and ram, roadblocks appear at 3 stars, a police helicopter hunts you at 4–5. Break line of sight and the heat cools off.
- **5 story missions**: Wheels, The Pickup, The Harbor Job, Clean Getaway, One Last Getaway — plus taxi fares, stunt jumps and 12 hidden sun coins.
- Progress (cash, missions, coins) saves in your browser.
